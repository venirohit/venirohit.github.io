About

About me

I'm a network engineer who likes owning a service end to end: setting its architecture, shipping it into production, and staying on the hook for how it runs. Most of my career has been spent on networks where a bad change reaches thousands of customers, and that has shaped how I work. I plan rollouts to be hitless, and I automate whatever I'd otherwise do by hand twice.

I started at Microsoft supporting Azure network infrastructure, spent five years helping build Oracle Cloud's networking from the early days, brought up the Edge and Core network for Meta's Phoenix data center, and returned to OCI to lead FastConnect. Lately my focus has been connectivity for AI and GPU workloads, and using LLM agents to take the toil out of network operations.
